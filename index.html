Who we are

Your trusted destination for advanced dental care

Welcome to Dentist & Co, Sydney's destination for advanced and comprehensive dental care. As a dual-accredited dental facility, we take pride in maintaining exceptional standards of infection control, hygiene, and patient safety.

Our team of independent clinicians includes experienced Dentists and Oral Health Therapists who are recognised experts in their respective fields. With a passion for excellence, they utilise the latest dental technologies and innovations to deliver safe, efficient, and personalised treatments.

Our highly trained team is dedicated to making every visit pleasant and stress-free. We believe in creating an environment where patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident about their dental care.

At Dentist & Co, building lasting relationships through trust, transparency, and genuine care is at the heart of everything we do.

Accreditation

Committed to the highest standards of patient safety

Dentist & Co is dual-accredited by QIP (Quality Innovation Performance), an independent recognition of our commitment to maintaining high facility standards and delivering quality dental care.

Our accreditation reflects the integration of National Safety and Quality Health Service Standards (NSQHS) into our everyday clinical practices, with a strong focus on patient safety, infection control, and continuous improvement.
